## Incantation
Recite a fairly long text to cast a spell.
Takes time to cast depending on the length of the text.
Player's velocity reduced while casting.
Can be interrupted. Either by external interaction or if the player do another action. But the cast process can be resumed immediately within a very short delay. The length of that delay depends on the spell and the player attributes.
The spell's attributes is calculated from the player's attributes and player's equipements.
Casting require that the player has sufficient mana and affinity level matching the spell.
Learned by reading it books (or any text) or taught by someone.
Can also be learned by witnessing someone else casting it sufficient number of time. That number depends on the length of the text and the complexity of the spell.

## Magic Circle
Draw symbols inside a circle to define a spell then activate it.
Casted in an instant. Only need to be activated.
Can be casted as many time as needed as long as the Player has the required amount of mana and affinity level matching the spell.
Can be customized.
Can be mounted between them to add more depth to the customization.
Can be drawn beforehand on almost anything. Paper, clothes, wall, ground, ...
Requires special ink materials to draw.
The spell's attributes is calculated from the drawing attributes.
The spell's attributes are not affected by player's attributes and player's equipements.
The object the spell is drawn on may affect the spell's attributes.
The ink material may affect the spell's attributes.
Takes time to draw depending on the complexity of the spell.
The drawing process can be interrupted and resumed at any time.
The drawing can be changed at any time.
Drawing one requires the player to learn spell attributes's symbol, pattern, and logic.
Spell attributes's symbol and pattern can be learned through books (or any text) or taught by someone.
Spell attributes's logic requires the player to test things around.
The drawing can also be copied by any player that have view-access to it even if the player doesn't understanding it.
The copy process takes time depending on the complexity of the spell.

## Spell Cast
Build/compose a spell (in the player's mind) then cast it.
Takes time to build depending on the complexity of the spell.
Building/composing a spell means "choosing" all it's attributes. No drawing.
Spell attributes are customized on the go.
Casting one works the same way as building a magic circle but instead of drawing you build it on the go.
Have to be rebuilt each time.
The spell's attributes symbol, pattern and logic are the exact same used in magic circle.
Learned by learning magic circle's symboll, pattern and logic.
A player can only build a single spell at a time.
A player may not cast a built spell and just "keep it in mind" to be used later in a short delay.
A player may "keep in mind" many spells to be able to "combine" them or to cast them multiple times without rebuilding them.
The amount of time a player "remembers" a built spell depends on the player's attributes.
The amount of spells a player can "remember" depends on the player's attributes.
The spell's attributes are calculated from the player's attribute and player's equipements.
